# FX builder brief: cinematic-reel effects library

Project: `videos/fx-showcase/`, a HyperFrames project at 1080×1920 and 30 fps, 34.4 s long. It is a showcase reel that demos 12 new creative effects. Each effect will later be copied into the `cinematic-reel` skill as a reusable template, so build every file to be **reused**: clean, commented and driven by a CONFIG block.

The lead has built the main timeline (`index.html`, generated by `build/build.py`). It holds the voice (take clips), the HUD label (`FX 0n / NAME`), documentary subtitles, grain and a slot for each effect. The slots are stubs in `compositions/fx/*.html`. Replace the stubs you are assigned.

## Read first

1. `.claude/skills/cinematic-reel/references/style.md`: palette, type, grade, motion and sound. These tokens are locked.
2. `.claude/skills/cinematic-reel/references/modules.md`: module 5 (behind-head word), which is the base of most face effects.
3. `../style-samples/cinematic-doc/compositions/board.html`: the approved evidence board, the quality bar for anything paper.
4. `~/.claude/skills/hyperframes-core/SKILL.md` and `references/variables-and-media.md`: the composition contract and media inside sub-compositions.
5. Reference screenshots the user wants these effects to resemble, described below. Translate each into OUR style (ink, film white, ONE red accent, Kalam/Tillana hand notes, paper textures), not their yellow-pill style.

## What the references show (the user's targets)

- **Chart grow:** a line chart with a grid and axis labels is drawn on the wall *behind* the speaker's head. The line grows upward to a glowing end point with a label pill ("SCALE ↗"). The speaker's cut-out stays in front.
- **Cover story:** a magazine cover. A huge serif masthead ("EDIT") sits *behind* the head, with a thin inset border frame. The footage itself is otherwise normal.
- **Frame break:** the speaker's footage plays inside a floating vertical reel card (rounded corners, small like/comment/share icons, username + Follow) in a dark, softly blurred space. His hands and arms break out past the card's edge, because the cut-out is drawn over the card border.
- **Headline wall:** the speaker sits in front of a wall of paper headline clippings and search-bar bubbles that replaces the room.
- **Couch wall:** the speaker sits in front of a grid wall of thumbnails of his own videos, with small view counts.
- **Paper cut:** a white sticker outline (a thick white border hugging the body silhouette) around the speaker, like a paper cut-out, with a hand-drawn doodle sticker (a heart and sparkle lines) beside him.
- **Doodle boil:** hand-drawn doodles that "boil": the stroke is re-posed every few frames, like real hand animation.
- **Phone mockup:** a phone floats beside the speaker's head, slightly tilted, showing an app UI card relevant to the line.
- **Vertigo, orbit, zoom-through, pull-back, paper tear:** no screenshot, so use the definitions below.

## File contract (must follow exactly)

- Edit only your assigned `compositions/fx/<file>.html` files, plus new assets under `assets/fx/<effect>/` if you need extracted stills. Never edit `index.html`, `build/`, or other agents' files. Do not render. Do not run `npx hyperframes check`.
- Root element: `<div id="<slot>-root" data-composition-id="<slot>" data-width="1080" data-height="1920">` inside `<template>`. Register the timeline as `window.__timelines["<slot>"]`, with one paused timeline. Prefix every id and class with a unique short prefix for your effect (for example `cv-` for cover).
- Put **all editable values in a `CONFIG` object at the top of the script**: texts, local cue times, colours that may vary, counts. Comment each one. Someone reusing the effect should only need to edit CONFIG, plus the media attributes, which are also commented.
- Times inside the comp are **local** (0 = slot start). The cue times below are already local.
- Face effects need the footage inside the comp. Use muted `<video class="clip">` elements with local `data-start="0"`, `data-duration` = the slot duration, and `data-media-start` as given below. Use `src="assets/take.mp4"` for the plate, and the matte webm (VP9 with alpha) for the cut-out, both muted. The root already plays the voice, so never put audio in your comp. Copy the grade attribute from `build/grading.attr` into `data-color-grading="…"` on every take/matte video you place, so they match the root.
- Layering for "behind the head": take plate → your background graphics → matte `.take-fg` copy → your foreground graphics. The matte picks up some curtain on the left of frame. Mask the matte with `clip-path: polygon(330px 0, 1080px 0, 1080px 1920px, 0 1920px, 0 1080px, 330px 1080px)` unless your layout needs something else.
- Keep text out of the root HUD band (y 100–170) and the subtitle band (y 1436–1576).
- Fonts: declare `@font-face` for every local font you use (`assets/type/Anton.woff2`, `IBMPlexSans.woff2`, `SpecialElite.woff2`, `assets/fonts/Kalam-700.woff2`, `Tillana-700.woff2`). Playfair Display and IBM Plex Mono load from Google by name. Kalam and Tillana are the only handwriting fonts.
- Keep it deterministic: no unseeded randomness, no clocks, no network. For boil jitter, derive the pose from quantised timeline time with a seeded hash.
- Never use yellow, gold, neon or a second accent. Never use bounce, `back.out`, shake or spin. Motion is calm, confident and filmic. Never use real logos or brand names (no Instagram/TikTok marks; generic icons only).

## Sound

Do not add `<audio>`. In your report, give SFX cues in **root time** as JSON, for example `{"t": 9.98, "file": "woosh", "volume": 0.9, "dur": 1.1}`. Limits: at most 2 cues per effect, and at most ONE click-type cue (`click` 2.0, `click-soft` 1.0, `mech-click` 3.5) per effect. Available files are in `assets/sfx/`: woosh (0.8–1.0), whoosh-short (0.55–0.85), riser-sharp (2.0–2.5), camera-flash (2.5), click, click-soft, mech-click.

## Verify (the machine is slow, so be economical)

1. `npx hyperframes lint`: 0 errors in your files.
2. `npx hyperframes snapshot --at <root times> --no-end --timeout 300000`: 3–5 times per effect. Read the contact sheet and open full frames where needed. Check the layering (head in front), the outline quality, that no text is clipped, readability, and that the HUD and subtitle bands are clear.
3. At most 4 snapshot rounds per effect.

## Report (final message)

Per effect:
1. A 3-line description.
2. The beat list (local and root times).
3. The SFX JSON.
4. The snapshot path.
5. Known limits.

**Also write `fx-notes/<effect>.md` for each effect**, about 15–25 lines, for the skill library:
- What it is.
- When to use it (what kind of spoken line triggers it).
- What it needs (matte? which media; slot duration range).
- How to reuse it (copy the file, edit CONFIG and the media attributes).
- Timing rules.
- SFX.
- Pitfalls.
